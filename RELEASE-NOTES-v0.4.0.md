First public version.

- Turns the item information window into clear sections: live stats, refine now and at the next level, effects, random options, expandable cards, and requirements with a level check.
- Compares base values and refine with your equipped item, clearly labelled, when the game opens its comparison window.
- Shows the copies your character carries, whether they're bound or can be traded, and the game's own restrictions.
- Works in renewal and pre-renewal, on a normal player account. If it hits a problem with an item, that window goes back to the game's normal look.
